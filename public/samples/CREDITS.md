Folder to General MIDI programme: `upright` bright acoustic piano, `epiano` electric piano 1,
`acguitar` steel acoustic guitar, `elguitar` clean electric guitar, `violin`, `flute`,
`sax` alto sax, `strings` string ensemble 1, `bass` fingered electric bass,
`bells` tubular bells, `veena` sitar (used as the closest openly licensed relative of the veena).

Only every third semitone is included; the player pitch-shifts between them.
No files were modified.
